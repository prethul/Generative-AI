Virat Kohli is not just a name in cricket; he is an emotion for millions of cricket fans around the world. Even after spending so many years at the highest level of international cricket, he continues to show that his hunger for runs and passion for the game have not faded. In his most recent ODI against New Zealand on November 4, 2026, Kohli delivered another outstanding performance, scoring 74 runs off just 51 deliveries, including 13 boundaries and one six. It was his 80th half-century in ODI cricket, and his aggressive batting played a crucial role in India's successful chase of a revised target of 143 runs, helping India secure a five-wicket victory. But his recent performance is only one part of the bigger story. Looking at his last two years in ODI cricket, his numbers are truly impressive. In 2025, Kohli scored 651 runs in 13 innings at an outstanding average of 65.10, including three centuries and four half-centuries. He finished the year as India's leading ODI run-scorer, proving that he could still dominate international bowling attacks. And in 2026, he has continued that remarkable consistency. As of November 4, he has scored 626 runs in 10 ODI innings at an average of 69.56, with two centuries and four half-centuries. That means he has already accumulated 1,277 ODI runs across these two calendar years, with five centuries and eight half-centuries. One of the most remarkable things about Kohli is not just his ability to score big hundreds but also his ability to perform under pressure, control the pace of an innings, build partnerships, and take responsibility when his team needs him the most. His fitness, discipline, dedication, and competitive mentality have also made him an inspiration for the next generation of cricketers. His greatness was officially recognised in 2020 when the International Cricket Council awarded him the prestigious Sir Garfield Sobers Award for ICC Male Cricketer of the Decade, making him the ICC Men's Cricketer of the Decade. He also received the ICC Men's ODI Cricketer of the Decade award. During that decade, he scored 20,396 international runs, the highest aggregate by any player in that period. He was also part of India's historic victories in the 2011 Cricket World Cup and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy. From chasing impossible-looking targets to delivering unforgettable performances on the biggest stages, Kohli has built a legacy that goes far beyond statistics. Many great players have come and gone, but very few have managed to maintain this level of consistency, passion, and commitment for such a long time. And perhaps the most emotional part for cricket lovers is that we are now witnessing the later chapters of an extraordinary career. We do not know how many more years we will get to watch him play at this level, so every innings, every cover drive, every century, and every celebration has become more precious. As fans, all we can do is enjoy every moment while the King is still out there, creating memories that will be remembered for generations.
